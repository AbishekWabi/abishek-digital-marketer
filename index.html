LEAD GENERATION · 6 MIN READ

Lead Generation: From First Ad to Relevant Enquiry

A lead campaign is only useful when the enquiries are relevant, so I look at lead quality as closely as lead count.

Defining the right enquiry

Before a campaign starts, I note what a good lead means for that business: the course, the property type, the service or the role.

Reviewing leads after they arrive

Enquiries are checked and marked as relevant or not, then compared by ad and audience to see which ones bring the right people.

Adjusting the campaign

Budget moves toward the ads and audiences that bring relevant enquiries, and the rest is paused or changed. My campaign projects show the kind of work this involves.

SEO · 5 MIN READ

Keyword Research: Finding Local Search Terms

Keyword research shows the words people use when they look for a service, and it decides what each page and ad says.

Customer language

Customers rarely use industry terms. A parent says "tuition near me" and a shop owner says "how to get more enquiries", so the page matches that wording.

Search intent

"What is SEO" is a learning search, while "SEO freelancer Nagercoil" is a hiring search. Each one needs its own page.

Local terms

Town and district names help a page compete locally. They are used naturally in headings and descriptions and not repeated for the sake of it.
